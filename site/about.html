About

What Brivia Club is.

Brivia Club is a networking app that introduces you to people who care about the same things you do, starting with the people near you.

How it works

You pick your interests and give more weight to the ones that matter most. We then show you members with similar interests in your neighbourhood first, then your city, then your region.

You can also search for anyone, anywhere, and send them a request. A conversation only opens when both people agree. We never show your exact location, only a rounded distance. Members must be 18 or older.

Signing in with Google

You can create an account with your email or with your Google account. If you choose Google, Google shares only your name, email address and profile picture with us, and we use them to set up your Brivia Club account. We do not post to your Google account or read anything else from it.
