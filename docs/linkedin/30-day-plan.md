  needs both people to agree.
- Link to thebriviaclub.com goes in the **first comment**, not the body. 0 to 2 niche hashtags.
- Images are real: photos, screenshots, screen recordings. No AI-generated people or events.

## Reaching people outside Golu's network

LinkedIn shows posts to non-connections mainly through comments, reposts and topic relevance, so most of the outside
reach comes from what Golu does *around* his posts:

1. **The first hour after each post:** reply to every comment within 60 minutes. Ask Yashika to leave a real comment
   (not "great post") early, and to repost posts 1, 4 and 8 with her own line on top: her 138-reaction audience is
   the biggest outside network we have.
2. **10 to 15 comments a day on other people's posts, about 30 minutes**, before or after posting:
   - 70% peers: Indian student devs and hackathon winners posting projects; hackathon organisers' announcement posts
     (Devfolio, MLH, Unstop, campus clubs). Add something useful, such as a tip or your experience, not a pitch.
   - 20% bigger names: Indian startup and student-community creators. Be among the first comments, with a specific
     point.
   - 10% prospects: people posting "looking for teammates", "new in Bangalore/Delhi", "anyone going to <event>?".
     Reply helpfully. Mention Brivia only where it truly answers the post, at most once a day.
3. **Weekly:** run `linkedin-engager-analytics` on the week's best post, then send connection requests with a note
   to the non-connections who engaged and fit the audience.
4. **Every few days:** run `linkedin-thread-monitor` to follow up on comment threads where an author replied.
5. **Profile:** headline mentions "Developer, Brivia Club" (never "co-founder"). Featured: Yashika's launch post and
   thebriviaclub.com. (`linkedin-profile-optimizer` can rewrite it.)

## Reaching founders and investors (for funding)

- **Hashtags:** 3–4 per post, mixing reach and audience: #buildinpublic #startupindia #founders plus one topic tag
  (#networking, #hackathon, #postgres, #productdesign). More than 5 reads as spam and lowers reach; in 2026 LinkedIn
  matches posts to readers mostly by what the text is about, so tags help a little and the story does most of it.
- **Every post says plainly what Brivia is and that it is early**, so an angel who sees it knows what they are
  looking at. Post 1 ends with an open invitation to people who back early community products.
- **Comment where investors read:** 3–5 thoughtful comments a day on posts by Indian angels, early-stage VCs and
  founders of community or social products (founder-led consumer apps, college-network startups). Add a real point
  from building Brivia; never pitch in a comment.
- **Warm paths:** ask Yashika and upGrad colleagues to repost post 1; tag only people who are genuinely part of a post.
- **Weekly:** run `linkedin-engager-analytics` on the best post and flag any founders or investors who engaged, so
  Golu can send a connection note.
