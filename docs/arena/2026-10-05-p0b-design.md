### R4. report_member (C's block rule, B's qualification rule)
- **Order (binding):** (1) profile required, else P0002; (2) **charge the cap first** in sender-only
  `report_attempt` (10 / rolling 24 h → PT429); (3) validate reason (22023): harassment, **explicit** (A-I5), spam,
  fake, underage, safety, other; (4) self or unknown id → return; (5) **block** reporter→target `on conflict do nothing`
  for **any existing profile, either world**, exactly what a direct block insert already allows (C-I1a); (6) same pair
  within 24 h → return; (7) insert the report.
- **member_report:** `reporter_id` → profiles `on delete set null`; `target_id` **no FK** (B-C1/C-I3); note ≤ 500,
  control characters stripped; `evidence jsonb` = last 50 messages of the pair (role, type, body, time, attachment path
  as reference) (B-C3); `qualifying boolean`; owner-only; indexes (reporter_id, created_at), target_id, created_at.
- **Qualifying (B-I1):** reporter completed, same world, account ≥ 7 d, and a **relation** (interaction, request,
  match or message, either direction). Others go to founder review only; incomplete accounts can still report.
- **Flag:** `'reported'` at **≥ 2 distinct qualifying reporters in 30 d**, `on conflict do nothing`; new
  `member_flag.expires_at` (auto flags 90 d, purged; founder flags null).
- **Underage (B-I2/C-I2):** one qualifying report → `suspended_pending_review` (never overrides 'restricted');
  `brivia_member_completed` excludes it; published 72 h founder review.
- **Client (A-I4/I5):** the card's decorative `•••` becomes a real 44×44 overflow (Report, Block), no third round
  button; chat `•••` gains "Report and block"; real dialog (focus trap, Escape, focus return); human reason labels; 112
  line for safety/underage; deck advances like a Pass; A's toast and PT429 copy; Blocked users notes "Unblocking
  doesn't cancel a report"; note "kept up to a year".
- **AC:** `brivia_blocks` snapshot identical after same-world vs other-world reports; unknown id writes nothing; 11th
  call → PT429 even after 10 no-ops; duplicates no-op; 1 qualifying report does not flag, 2 do and the target leaves
  `refresh_cell_density`; non-qualifying never flags; underage → invisible; evidence ≤ 50; reports survive the

### R6. Delete-and-rejoin evasion (B-C1 partly adopted; Vault deferred)
- **When it applies.** Only when the deleting member has a report against them or a `member_flag`.
- **What is written.** `moderation_tombstone(digest, reasons, report_ids, deleted_at, expires_at = +365 d)`.
  - `digest = hmac(lower(email), pepper, 'sha256')`.
  - The pepper is generated once by 0005 (`gen_random_bytes`) into an owner-only table. It is **not in Vault**: proportionate pre-beta, same trust boundary as the DB owner.
- **On rejoin.** `declare_adult` computes the digest of the caller's auth email. On a match it inserts `member_flag('rejoin_review')`, which drops the member from density, and the founder reviews. There is no refusal and no visible difference.
- **AC.** A reported member deletes, re-signs up with the same email, declares, and gets flagged. An unreported member leaves no tombstone. The purge removes tombstones after 365 days.

## Deferred to P1 (with reason)
- **Identity tombstone keyed by phone and provider ids, pepper in Vault:** needs a Vault secret and the founder's setup. R6 covers email.
- **Storage insert policies checking that the auth user still exists (B-I3):** the 1-hour JWT window, plus uploads into the member's own folder, is low harm. The cost is a rewrite of 8 storage policies on live. Instead, the runbook adds a nightly orphan-folder report query.
- **`storage_gc` queue for attachments orphaned by a counterpart's deletion:** disclosed for now.
- **An Edge Function deletion that is atomic with storage:** needs a deploy and a secret.
- **Inactive-account deletion with notice:** needs email sending.
- **Moderation tooling and an appeal flow:** the founder works from the dashboard SQL for now.
- **Old photos on non-replace paths, and the 12 px rule app-wide.**
- **Reporter account age for open sign-ups:** revisit 7 days against 14 days with real data.

## Dissent preserved
- **Packet (withdraw → recompletion).** Re-spending reflects true intent. *Overruled:* DPDP requires that withdrawing be as easy as giving consent. Redistribution keeps the member visible, and they can still edit afterwards.
- **Packet (perf rewrite inside 0005).** One apply is simpler. *Overruled:* isolating the riskiest mechanical change makes rollback and the drift proof cleaner.
- **B-C1 (identity HMAC over email, phone and provider ids, key in Vault, for every deletion).** *Partly overruled:* the scope is limited to reported or flagged members, and to email, with a DB-held pepper until counsel answers the 180-day question.
- **B (relation required before writing a block or a report).** *Overruled:* C's "block any existing id" adds nothing a direct block insert doesn't already reveal, and it is simpler. B's relation test is kept for flag qualification.
- **B-I3 (career-resumes in the emptiness check).** *Overruled:* recruiting is a separate purpose. Making it block deletion would trap applicants, who cannot list or delete those files.
- **C-I2 (14-day reporter age).** The judge chose B's 7 days. During the closed beta the founder reviews every report by hand anyway.
- **C-I4 (suffix in `set_home_city` too).** *Overruled* in favour of a client append, to avoid redefining a frozen function body.

### critic-B

### Critic B: P0-B design (privacy, security, abuse, DPDP lens)

Grounded in 0001-0004, the harness stub, supabase.js and script.js. I made no probes against any remote database.

#### Critical

**C1. Deleting an account destroys the evidence of abuse, and the member can rejoin with a clean record.**
`member_report.target_id` uses `on delete cascade`, and so does `member_flag` (0004:887). `brivia_messages`
sender_id and recipient_id also cascade (0001:195-196). A harasser who calls delete_my_account removes the reports
about them, their flag, and every message they sent to the victim. They can then sign up again with the same email.
*Fix:* (a) Change `target_id` to `on delete set null` and add `target_ref uuid` (no FK), so the report survives.
(b) Before the cascade runs, delete_my_account copies any flag or report about the caller into
`moderation_tombstone(identity_hmac, reasons, report_ids, expires_at = now()+1y)`. The identity_hmac is an HMAC of
lower(email), phone and the provider identity ids, with the key held in Vault. (c) Check the tombstone in
declare_adult or at completion. (d) Disclose this retention in privacy.html as "retention needed for safety or by
law". DPDP s.8(7) permits retention that is required for compliance with law.

**C2. report_member works as an existence oracle.**
The design blocks reporter->target only when the report is accepted. Members can read their own blocks
(0001:139-141 `blocker_id = auth.uid()`). So a real, same-world target leaves a visible block row, while an unknown
or other-world id leaves none. That leaks whether an id exists and whether it is a test account (Ruling P14). The cap
has the same problem if no-ops are not charged: when the 11th call returns PT429, the caller learns that the first 10
ids were real.
*Fix:* Follow Ruling A1. Charge the cap on every call, before any check on the target. Accept a target only when the
reporter has a historical relation with it: an impression, interaction, request, match or message in either
direction. Current brivia_visible_to is the wrong test, because when the target has blocked the reporter, a missing
block row would tell the reporter "they blocked me". When the relation exists, always write the block, since the id
is already known to the reporter. When it does not, do nothing and write no block. Add a harness test that compares
`select * from brivia_blocks` after a report on a real target and after one on an unknown id.

**C3. Reports keep no evidence, and the reported person can erase it.**
Messages are not tied to matches and cascade with either profile. Nothing captures what was reported.
*Fix:* When a report comes from chat, report_member copies the last N (for example 50) messages of the pair into
`member_report.evidence jsonb`. Attachment paths are copied as references only. The evidence follows the C1 retention
and is visible only to the owner.

#### Important

**I1. Weaponised reporting.** Today one report flags the target and drops them from density. Flags never expire and
there is no appeal. One account can file 10 reports a day and push a cell below k. Hysteresis then resets the 7-night
streak for every member in that cell, which degrades location for the whole neighbourhood. *Fix:* Record every report,
but count it toward member_flag only when the reporter is completed, adult-declared, has an account older than 7
days, and has the C2 relation. Set the flag only at 2 or more distinct qualifying reporters, or after the founder
confirms. Add `flag_expires_at`. Upsert with `on conflict do nothing` so that 'reported' never overwrites a stronger
moderator reason. Let incomplete accounts report (a minor being harassed should be able to), but their reports go to
the review queue only.

**I2. An 'underage' report changes nothing for the target.** A flag affects only density counts, so the reported
member stays visible and matchable. ORBIT taste learning on a child counts as behavioural monitoring under DPDP
s.9(3). *Fix:* A qualifying 'underage' report sets `member_flag.reason='suspended_pending_review'`, and
brivia_member_completed must exclude that reason. Publish a review SLA, for example 72 h.

**I3. Deletion leaves data behind.**
- A JWT stays valid for up to 1 h after `delete from auth.users`. The storage insert policies check only
  `foldername[1] = auth.uid()` (0001 storage section), and storage.objects has no FK to auth.users. A deleted user can
  therefore still upload into a public bucket. *Fix:* Storage insert policies also require a security-definer
  `brivia_auth_user_exists()`.
- The emptiness check uses folders only. Also check `owner_id = auth.uid()::text` in every bucket, including
  career-resumes: Storage sets owner_id for authenticated uploads, so resumes can be linked after all. The harness stub
  has no `owner_id` column and no protect_delete trigger, so the proposed tests would not exercise what runs live. Add
  both to the stub.
- Storage `list()` returns 100 entries per page and does not recurse. Page through the results, and give a fallback
  ("email us") when storage_not_empty persists.
- When the deleted member's messages cascade, attachments in the *sender's* folder lose every reference to them, and
  those images can show the deleted member. Queue those paths in a `storage_gc` table for an owner sweep. Run a
  nightly report of objects whose folder uid is not in auth.users.
- Not deleted by SQL: `auth.audit_log_entries` (no FK, and holds email and IP), Supabase platform logs, PITR and daily
  backups, the CDN cache of public photo URLs, and `brivia-member-profile` in localStorage on other devices.
  privacy.html must disclose all of these with their windows.
- Typing "DELETE" does not prove who is asking. A stolen session (for example via XSS in the vanilla-JS DOM code)
  can erase the account irreversibly. *Fix:* Require a recent authentication: the amr timestamp in `auth.jwt()` must
  be within the last 10 minutes, otherwise refuse with `reauth_required`. Spoofing auth.uid() itself is not a risk:
  PostgREST sets it, and the function takes no uid parameter. Keep it that way and assert `uid is not null`.

**I4. Consent cannot be proven (DPDP s.6(10) puts the burden of proof on the fiduciary).** Both adult_declared_at and
sensitive_consent_at are single timestamps, and withdrawal nulls the second, so no history survives. *Fix:* Add an
append-only `consent_event(member_id, kind in ('adult','sensitive_give','sensitive_withdraw'), notice_version, at)`.
`declare_adult(p_notice_version)` should reject a version that is not current, so the record shows which text the
member affirmed. On account deletion, keep only the events, keyed by the identity HMAC, for the retention period.

**I5. Server-side enforcement of the 18+ gate is sound.** Every visibility and consent path goes through
brivia_member_completed and brivia_visible_to (0004:696-737), and brivia_has_completed_profile was redefined (0004
§6). The completion path loops back to step 1. Self-declaration is the ceiling of what this can prove; that is
acceptable if I2 is in place. The remaining gap is defence in depth: both columns rely only on the column grant on
UPDATE. *Fix:* Add a BEFORE UPDATE guard, in the style of brivia_guard_is_test, that preserves `adult_declared_at` and
`sensitive_consent_at` unless the session is the owner. A future `grant update on profiles` would otherwise make both
columns writable by members.

**I6. Rewriting the policies is risky.** Permissive policies are OR'd together, so a mistyped `drop policy if exists`
name leaves the old policy live next to the new one. Live may also hold policies created in the dashboard. *Fix:* Put
the 17 rewrites in a separate migration (0006). Add a harness test that snapshots `pg_policies` (table, name, cmd,
roles, permissive, and qual / with_check with `( SELECT auth.uid() AS uid)` normalised to `auth.uid()`) and asserts it
is equal before and after, with the same count per table. Before applying, diff live `pg_policies` against the
migrations read-only.

**I7. The retention schedule is incomplete.** Missing entries:
- career-resumes have no retention at all. Suggest 180 d, linked by owner_id.
- member_flag has no expiry.
- Messages, matches and posts are kept for the life of the account. State this.
- No inactive-account policy. Suggest 3 y with 48 h notice, aligned with the DPDP Rules Third Schedule.
- Backup, log and audit windows (see I3).

Check log retention: DPDP Rules 2025 r.8(3) requires processing logs for at least 1 y, and CERT-In (2022) requires
ICT logs for 180 d in India. Supabase's own log retention is far shorter. Verify both against the notified text and
keep a minimal processing log (tombstones and consent_event) to satisfy them.

**I8. The breach runbook needs real content.** It should cover:
- Board intimation without delay, plus a detailed report **within 72 h** (r.7(2)).
- A notice to members in plain language: nature, extent, timing, likely consequences, mitigation, steps the member
  can take, and a contact.
- CERT-In within 6 h (incident@cert-in.org.in).
- Escalation to Supabase and Vercel as processors.
- **Pause the purge cron** to preserve evidence.
- Rotate the JWT secret (this revokes all sessions), the service-role key, the DB password, Vercel env vars and
  GitHub tokens. Confirm 2FA on the Gmail grievance inbox.
- An owner, a decision log and ready-made templates.

- M1. Note content: strip control characters, store it in a column members cannot read, and say in the dialog that it
  is kept for 365 d.
- M2. Recording consent only when a sensitive interest is picked is good minimisation. However, if
